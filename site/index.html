            About.

            The CA-44 Atlas is a nonpartisan, sourced record of a seat in Congress. It follows the seat, not any one officeholder.

            Publisher. Thomas J. Norman publishes the atlas as a personal project and is responsible for its contents.

            Independent. The atlas is not a product of the Northwest San Pedro Neighborhood Council, of California State University, Dominguez Hills, or of any other government body, employer, campaign, party, or officeholder. It endorses no candidate and takes no position on legislation.

            Sources. Every figure comes from a public record named in the Sources section. Estimates by the atlas are marked "est."

            Corrections. If a number or label looks wrong, report it with a link to the primary source. Accepted corrections are noted in the changelog.

            Reuse. Code: MIT. Text and compiled tables: CC BY 4.0. The code, the data files, and the method are in the public repository.
